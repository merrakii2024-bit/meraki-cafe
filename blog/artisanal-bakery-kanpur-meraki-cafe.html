While our 72-hour cold-fermented pizzas and stone-rolled ice creams have captured headlines across Kanpur, true dessert and pastry lovers know that the heart of Meraki Café beats loudest in our bakery kitchen. Tucked away in Saket Nagar, our counter is a daily celebration of butter, flour, patience, and pure soul.

If you thought Meraki was just a place for an evening boba or an orange mojito, it is time to discover our freshly baked creations crafted from scratch every single morning.

The Philosophy of Our Oven

True to our Greek namesake—meraki (doing something with soul, creativity, and absolute love)—our baking process refuses shortcuts. We do not believe in mass-produced commercial mixes or frozen dough blocks. Every loaf, bun, croissant, and tart is scaled, shaped, and baked in small batches daily.

When you step through our doors in Saket Nagar, that unmistakable aroma of caramelized sugar and toasted butter isn't an accident; it is our daily invitation to slow down and savour the moment.

"True baking is an act of devotion. You cannot rush butter and flour; you give them time, care, and attention, and they reward you with magic." — Meraki Café Bakery Team

Bakery Highlights — What to Try From the Oven

Our bakery counter rotates fresh batches daily, featuring 100% vegetarian creations made with premium ingredients and traditional techniques. Here are the guest favourites you need to try on your next visit:

1

Artisanal Sourdough & Seeded Loaves

Crusty on the outside, custardy and bubbly on the inside. Our slow-fermented sourdough utilizes wild yeasts to develop a deep, complex flavor profile that elevates everything from morning toast to evening panini pairings.

2

Flaky Butter Croissants

Rolling butter into dough is an act of sheer devotion. Our classic French croissants feature dozens of delicate, shatteringly crisp layers that yield an airy, buttery interior. Pair it with a hot cappuccino for the ultimate afternoon pause.

3

Signature Small-Batch Pastries & Tarts

From rich chocolate ganache tarts to seasonal fruit pastries loaded with fresh produce, our bakery case changes slightly based on what looks best at the morning market. Every item is crafted without compromise.
